Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Value

Founded in 2011 in Harare, One Stone Auto Parts started with a simple mission — to give Zimbabwean vehicle owners access to quality aftermarket parts at fair prices.

Over the years, we've become the country's go-to specialist for Nissan Caravan models (E24, E25, E26) and other Japanese vehicles. Today, with branches across Harare, we serve thousands of vehicle owners, mechanics, and fleet operators.

Our exclusive Kanagava brand represents everything we stand for — high-quality aftermarket parts tested for African driving conditions.
